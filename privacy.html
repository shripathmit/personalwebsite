Privacy

This is a personal site. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no forms. Here is what is collected when you visit, and why.

Analytics. The site uses two analytics tools. Plausible counts page views in aggregate and does not use cookies. Microsoft Clarity records anonymized session behavior such as clicks, scrolling, and heatmaps, and may set cookies in your browser to do so. I use both only to see which pages are useful and where the site is confusing.

Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google when a page opens.

Other sites. Links to Substack, GitHub, LinkedIn, and the kReative Labs apps leave this site. Each of those has its own privacy practices.

Contact. If you email me, I keep the message so I can reply. I do not add anyone to a list or share addresses.

Questions or removal requests. Write to shripathmit@gmail.com.
