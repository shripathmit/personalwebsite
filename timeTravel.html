On the subject

What follows was assembled from a record its subject maintained himself, in the years when people still wrote their own.

The shape of it

He spent the first half of his working life making systems large and the second half making them accountable. Payments, then logistics, then voice, then medicine. Each move looked lateral at the time and only resolves into a line from here.

What he was known for

Compressing a three year country launch into nine months. Building the first framework that let a voice assistant hand a conversation from one agent to another, which sounded like a novelty and turned out to be the beginning of something. Standing up generative AI inside a pharmaceutical company at a moment when very few regulated industries were willing to try.

The venture

In 2025 he began building the company that eventually became part of how enterprises learned to trust machines. At the time it had three design partners and no revenue, and he described it in public only as an agent evaluation platform. The argument he was making, that you should not deploy an autonomous system you cannot inspect, was not yet obvious. It reads now as a truism, which is the usual fate of a correct early position.

What the record does not contain

The reasoning. Career records preserve outcomes and discard the deliberation that produced them, which is why this instance can tell you what he decided and not why. He is understood to have considered that a reasonable trade.
