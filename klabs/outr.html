The problem

Good outreach takes hours nobody has

Researching a company enough to write something specific takes real time. Writing something that does not sound like the other two hundred emails in the same inbox takes longer. Founders either do it badly or do not do it at all, and both outcomes look the same from the recipient's side.

The obvious automation makes it worse. Volume tools solved the sending problem and made the reading problem everyone else's. The interesting constraint is doing the research well while keeping a person in control of what actually goes out.
