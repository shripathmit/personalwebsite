Taste Network

A human taste network that helps you choose what to ship. Upload two to five versions of something you're deciding on; real people compare them and explain why. No AI judges the work.

Try it live

The problem

A model can score your options. It cannot want one.

Founders, designers, and marketers ask AI to pick between two headlines, two landing pages, two logos, constantly. A model will always give an answer, confidently, and that answer is a guess about taste dressed up as a verdict. Taste is exactly the thing models are worst at, because it is not a fact to retrieve, it is a reaction a real person has.

The fix here is not a better judge. It is removing the judge: route the same two-to-five options to real humans, let them pick and say why, and treat the aggregate of those reactions as the signal, not a single model's opinion.

At a glance

StackStatic frontend, Node static server, Supabase (Postgres + Auth + Storage) as the backend.
Test flowUpload 2 to 5 versions of a decision; respondents compare, pick, and explain their choice; responses aggregate into a result.
Safety modelRow-level security scopes every test to its owner; environment secrets are injected server-side and never touch git.
